How a project runs

Ingest to

delivery.

The same order on every job, so nothing gets rebuilt twice and you always know where your video is.

01Ingest

Brief & Media

Footage organized by camera, notes read, story order mapped before the first cut.

02Edit

Story Cut

Pacing, B-roll and dialogue continuity. No jump cuts unless the style calls for them.

03Fairlight

Sound

Channel fixes, voice isolation, music under dialogue and SFX that follow the action.

04Color

Grade

Camera matching and a look that fits the content, with skin tones kept natural.

05Fusion

Motion

Titles, maps, logo reveals and compositing that serve the story.

06Deliver

QC & Export

Checked export, Shorts cut from the master and an organized project you can reopen.
